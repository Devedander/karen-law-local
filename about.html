Karen’s Approach

Karen Webb, Esq. specializes in bringing the law to your corner and listening to your concerns. She is dedicated to understanding what results you want and to helping you understand what actions we can take on your behalf. She will work with you every step of the way to make sure that you understand the choices you are making and feel empowered to make them.

Mediation is Karen’s specialty. You know the issues better then anyone else so why let a judge tell you what will happen in your case. Mediation is the best way to get the results you want because you get to be involved directly in the outcome. You might not get everything you want, but you will have a say in what happens. Let Karen help you negotiate your own settlement, on your own terms.

Before you file, try mediation! Give Karen a call to discuss it before you hire a trial lawyer.
